### （二）各章节核心功能
1. **引言（Introduction）**：以2023年温网经典赛事为切入点，明确网球动量的研究意义；澄清问题核心（量化动量、检验存在性、预测波动、泛化模型、提供策略）；概述研究方法（统计分析+机器学习+动态模拟）与目标。
2. **建模准备（Preparation for Modeling）**：设定简化模型的合理假设（排除次要变量、认可排名有效性）；定义关键符号便于后续计算；处理原始数据（异常值、无用列），为建模提供高质量数据基础。
3. **概率差异评估策略（Problem 1）**：解决“如何量化球员实时表现”的问题，通过发球方影响分析、滑动分点区间法、滑动时间窗口法（结合AUC指标），构建不受发球干扰的性能评估指标，精准捕捉球员表现波动。
4. **动量存在性（Problem 2）**：通过假设检验（Ljung-Box Q检验、游程检验）验证得分随机性，再结合瞬时胜率分析，论证“动量存在但不显著”的核心结论，为后续动量建模提供理论依据。
5. **动量预测（Problem 3）**：核心章节，先通过朴素二项式模型分离球员实力差异与发球优势，将残差定义为动量效应；再构建双时间贝叶斯网络（含潜在变量与显变量），通过EM算法训练模型，量化各因素对动量的影响并给出策略建议。
6. **波动预测与模型泛化（Problem 4）**：在温网决赛及多场赛事中验证模型有效性；拓展至女性网球赛事，分析性别差异；构建通用框架，实现对乒乓球、篮球等多运动场景的适配。
7. **模型分析（Model Analysis）**：客观评估模型优势（捕捉长短时动量、含潜在变量、可训练性强）与不足（潜在变量难验证、数据聚合损失粒度）；通过敏感性分析测试模型鲁棒性；总结研究核心成果。
8. **备忘录（Memorandum）**：将模型结论转化为教练可执行的策略（减少非受迫性失误、主动进攻、智能发球等），衔接理论研究与实际应用。

## 二、写作风格
### （一）常用过渡词/连接词
1. 逻辑递进：Further、Furthermore、In addition、Additionally、Moreover、Subsequently、Subsequently、To further clarify
2. 转折对比：However、But、On the contrary、In stark contrast、Unexpectedly
3. 因果关系：Thus、Therefore、Consequently、As a result、Due to、Owing to
4. 举例说明：For instance、For example、Specifically
5. 总结归纳：In summary、To summarize、Overall、Generally
6. 引出结论：Our findings suggest、This indicates、It reveals、As evidenced by

### （二）句子结构特点
- 以**被动语态为主**，突出研究对象和客观结论，减少主观视角。例：
  - "Rigorous hypothesis testing (Ljung-Box Q Test and Runs Test) fails to reject null hypothesis"（主动语态仅用于强调研究行为）
  - "The observed increase in winning rates by around 1% might indicate the influence of momentum"
  - "Variables are discretized to facilitate model training"
- 长句占比高，多包含从句（定语从句、状语从句、宾语从句）和插入语，精准表达复杂逻辑关系。例：
  - "Drawing from the insights of [Taylor, 1994], we see that momentum is a complex system of cognition, physiology, and emotion, all sparked by precipitating events and manifesting in performance changes."

### （三）数据呈现方式
- **图表优先，文字辅助**：核心数据通过表格（符号定义、特征重要性）、折线图（得分差异、概率差异）、热力图（特征相关性）呈现，文字仅用于解读图表含义、补充关键数值。
- 数据与结论绑定：每个图表后紧跟分析，明确数据反映的规律。例：
  - "From Figure 3, the part above the red line indicates that player 1 (Carlos Alcaraz) is better than player 2 (Nicolas Jarry)... the position of the peaks and valleys indicates that the player is performing particularly well."
- 关键数据单独强调：重要统计结果（如P值、参数估计值）以文字形式突出，例："the optimal value is computed as λ=0.0624, s=0.1742"、"P=0.691, which is greater than common significance levels (e.g., 0.05)"。

## 三、专业表达
### （一）数学建模领域专业术语（中英对照）
| 中文术语 | 英文术语 | 定义简述 |
|----------|----------|----------|
| 朴素二项式模型 | Naive Binomial Model | 假设得分独立同分布，仅考虑球员实力与发球优势的基础模型 |
| 双时间贝叶斯网络 | Dual-Temporal Bayesian Network | 融合长短时记忆，含潜在变量的概率生成模型，用于预测动量残差 |
| 滑动窗口法 | Sliding Window Method | 固定长度数据区间滚动计算，捕捉数据局部特征的方法 |
| 曲线下面积 | Area Under Curve (AUC) | 量化模型性能或数据累积效应的指标 |
| 假设检验 | Hypothesis Testing | 验证统计结论的方法（含Ljung-Box Q检验、游程检验） |
| 残差效应 | Residual Effect | 实际结果与模型预测值的差值，此处定义为动量效应 |
| 期望最大化算法 | Expectation-Maximization (EM) Algorithm | 处理潜在变量的参数估计算法，分E步（计算期望）和M步（最大化似然） |
| 信息增益/熵减 | Information Gain / Entropy Reduction | 衡量变量对模型预测不确定性降低程度的指标 |
| 敏感性分析 | Sensitivity Analysis | 测试模型参数或数据变化对结果影响的稳健性分析 |
| 独立同分布 | Independent and Identically Distributed (i.i.d.) | 数据满足独立性和相同分布的假设 |

### （二）描述模型时的固定句式
1. We develop/construct/propose a [模型名称] to [模型目的].  
   例："We develop a naive binomial model to separate the skill difference and serving advantage from momentum effect."
2. The [模型名称] incorporates/includes/encodes [核心要素] to [实现功能].  
   例："The Dual-Temporal Bayesian Network incorporates latent variables such as physiology and self-efficacy to capture complex momentum dynamics."
3. The model is trained/tested on [数据集] using [算法/方法].  
   例："We train the network on the data of 31 matches in Wimbledon 2023 using the EM algorithm."
4. The [模型组件] is defined as [数学表达式/定义], which [含义解释].  
   例："The momentum effect is defined as E = S - Ŝ, which represents the deviation of actual performance from the expected performance."
5. To address [问题], we adopt a [步骤/方法] approach: first [步骤1], then [步骤2].  
   例："To predict momentum, we take a 2-step approach: first separate out the momentum effect as residual, then develop a Bayesian Network to predict this residual."

### （三）分析结果时的常用表达
1. Our findings suggest/reveal/indicate that [结论].  
   例："Our findings suggest that proactive and aggressive playstyles significantly contribute to a player's momentum."
2. The results show/demonstrate that [现象/规律].  
   例："The results demonstrate that the model accurately forecasts most match fluctuations."
3. There is a significant correlation between [变量A] and [变量B].  
   例："Unforced errors show a significant correlation with the outcome of points."
4. [指标/数值] exceeds/is greater than/less than [基准值], indicating that [结论].  
   例："P-values exceed the 0.05 benchmark, indicating insufficient evidence to reject the null hypothesis."
5. [变量] exerts a positive/negative/influential impact on [研究对象].  
   例："Net point won exerts a positive influence on momentum, while unforced errors undermine it."

### （四）衔接图片和写作的套话
1. As depicted in Figure [编号], [图表内容描述], which [图表反映的结论].  
   例："As depicted in Figure 2, the points difference over time highlights the stable trend regardless of the server."
2. Figure [编号] illustrates/shows/depicts [核心信息].  
   例："Figure 8 illustrates the correlation between top-10 features predictive of point victor."
3. From Figure [编号], we can observe/determine that [结论].  
   例："From Figure 5, we can determine which player is performing better at a certain time."
4. The graph in Figure [编号] plots/resents [数据维度], where [坐标轴/图例解释] and [结论].  
   例："The graph in Figure 3 plots the probability difference M¹, where the abscissa is the total number of points and the ordinate represents M¹."

## 四、论证逻辑
### （一）如何引出模型假设
1. 明确假设目的：先说明假设是为了简化模型、聚焦核心因素，例："While these factors can influence a match, including them in a model adds complexity and unpredictability. For a broad analysis, it's practical to focus on more measurable and consistent factors."
2. 分点列出假设+合理性论证：每个假设后紧跟"Justification"，从数据可获得性、行业共识、研究范围等角度解释假设的合理性。例：
   - 假设2："A player's current world ranking is reliable indicators of their potential performance"
   - 论证："Rankings are based on a player's performance in tournaments over a rolling period, reflecting their overall ability."
3. 假设限定范围：明确假设适用场景，避免绝对化，例："For a broad analysis, it's practical to focus on more measurable and consistent factors"（限定于“广泛分析”场景）。

### （二）如何说明模型选择的合理性
1. 问题适配性：强调模型与研究问题的匹配度，例："Since momentum implies some 'hidden state', a generative model (Bayesian Network) is preferred."
2. 现有方法局限性+本模型优势：对比传统方法不足，突出所选模型的改进，例："Direct 1-step prediction can be extremely noisy; our Dual-Temporal structure encodes long-short term memory to reduce noise."
3. 理论支撑：引用相关文献论证模型有效性，例："Refer to [Klaassen and Magnus, 2001] for further justification of the rank-strength relationship in the binomial model."
4. 实证验证：通过数据拟合结果证明模型合理性，例："The optimal parameters λ=0.0624 and s=0.1742 are obtained by maximizing the log-likelihood, ensuring the binomial model fits the data well."

### （三）如何讨论模型局限性
1. 客观承认不足：分点明确列出局限性，不回避关键问题，例：
   - "Difficult to fully validate accuracy of latent variable representations without actual measurements of players' psychological states."
   - "Requires aggregation of data over intervals to reduce noise, losing some granularity."
2. 分析局限性原因：结合数据、方法、研究条件解释局限产生的合理性，例："Due to the limitation of our data (only 31 matches), a complicated model is prone to overfitting and thus not suitable."
3. 提出改进方向：针对每个局限性给出具体的未来优化方案，体现研究的延续性，例：
   - 针对“未考虑球员个人特征”："A data-based integration of player's personal information (e.g., surface preferences) may further boost our model's performance."
   - 针对“潜在变量难验证”："Future research could incorporate psychological measurement data to validate latent variable dynamics."
