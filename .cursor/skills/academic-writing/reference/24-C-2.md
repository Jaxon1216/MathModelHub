### （二）各章节核心功能
1. **Introduction（引言）**：阐述网球领域中动量问题的研究争议背景，综述相关文献观点；明确论文需解决的四大核心任务（比赛进程衡量、动量存在性验证、转折点预测、模型泛化）；概述整体研究框架与核心方法。
2. **Assumption（假设）**：提出三大关键假设（球员个人能力比赛中恒定、能力值可通过比赛数据推断、发球方具有优势），为模型构建简化分析场景，明确研究前提。
3. **Notations（符号说明）**：定义论文中核心变量与参数（如PE、PS、SA等），统一表述标准，为后续模型公式与分析提供清晰的符号支撑。
4. **Model I: 基于DPI的比赛进程衡量模型**：构建动态比赛指数（DPI），通过数据预处理解决缺失值问题，分别量化表现（PE）、个人实力（PS）、发球优势（SA）三大维度，确定权重并形成DPI计算公式；通过实际比赛数据验证模型对比赛进程的刻画能力。
5. **Task 2: 动量存在性验证**：采用反证法，假设动量不存在，基于高斯分布模拟比赛得分；运用动态时间规整（DTW）算法对比模拟得分与实际得分的相似性，通过模型预测结果与随机模拟结果的差异，证实动量的客观存在。
6. **Model II: 基于HMM-随机森林的动量转折点模型**：定义动量、动量转移、转折点等核心概念；利用随机森林模型筛选动量转移关键指标并预测转折点（准确率77.6%）；结合隐马尔可夫模型（HMM）分析球员个体对动量变化的反应差异；引入情境杠杆量化得分点重要性；输出预测结果并提出针对性教练建议。
7. **Task 4: 模型泛化性验证**：将模型应用于不同类型比赛（温网男单、女单、美网硬地赛），评估模型在不同场景下的预测效果，分析场地表面等因素对模型性能的影响，提出模型优化方向。
8. **Sensitivity Analysis（敏感性分析）**：通过对关键指标添加高斯噪声，测试模型召回率变化，验证模型的稳定性与鲁棒性。
9. **Strength and Weakness（模型优势与不足）**：总结模型的核心优势（多维度整合、贴合网球规则、融合两种算法优势）；指出局限性（HMM依赖历史数据、跨项目泛化能力弱），并提出未来改进方向。
10. **Memorandum for The Coaches（给教练的备忘录）**：将模型研究结果转化为实用教练策略，围绕得分连贯性、长回合耐力、关键击球、对手分析等维度提供训练建议，衔接理论研究与实际应用。
11. **References（参考文献）**：列出支撑研究的相关文献，增强论文的学术严谨性与可信度。
12. **Appendices（附录）**：补充随机森林模型训练所用的15项具体指标，提供数据支撑细节，方便读者追溯与验证。

## 二、写作风格
### （一）常用过渡词/连接词
1. 逻辑递进类：First（首先）、Then（随后）、After that（之后）、Next（接下来）、Finally（最后）、Furthermore（此外）、Moreover（此外）、Additionally（另外）
2. 因果关系类：Therefore（因此）、Consequently（因此）、Thus（因而）、As a result（结果）
3. 转折对比类：Conversely（相反地）、However（然而）、While（同时）、Except for（除……之外）
4. 举例说明类：For example（例如）、For instance（例如）、Specifically（具体而言）
5. 总结归纳类：In conclusion（总之）、In summary（综上）、To summarize（总结）

### （二）句子结构特点
- 以**被动语态为主**，强调研究过程与结果的客观性，减少主观表述。例如：“The model was validated using actual match data.”（模型通过实际比赛数据验证）、“Missing values were imputed using median values.”（缺失值通过中位数填充）。
- 长句占比较高，常包含多个从句（定语从句、状语从句、宾语从句），用于精准表述复杂逻辑关系（如模型原理、假设条件、数据处理流程）。例如：“To assess a player based on the current situation of the game, we establish a metric known as 'performance', which is defined as the chance of a player winning a game predicted by analyzing various situational factors through a Gradient Boosting Trees classification model.”

### （三）数据呈现方式
- 采用“**图表优先+文字补充+表格量化**”的组合方式，数据可视化程度高。
- 核心数据（如模型准确率、指标权重、相似性结果）通过表格精准呈现，方便对比；
- 动态变化过程（如DPI曲线、转折点分布）通过折线图、散点图直观展示；
- 文字部分仅对图表数据进行解读、分析因果关系或强调关键结论，不重复罗列原始数据。例如：“From the DTW Alignment Paths displayed in the upper part of the graph, it is evident that the curves of the randomly simulated sequences largely deviate from the diagonal, indicating a significant discrepancy from the actual sequence.”（通过图表观察到随机模拟序列与实际序列差异显著）。

### （二）描述模型时的固定句式
1. We develop/construct/establish [模型名称] to [模型用途], which integrates/employs [核心方法/维度] and is represented by the equation [公式].  
   示例：“We develop the Dynamic Play Index (DPI) to seek the dynamic flow of tennis, which integrates Performance (PE), Personal Strength (PS), and Serving Advantage (SA) and is represented by the equation \(DPI=\alpha × PE+\beta × PS+\gamma × SA\).”
2. Based on [理论/方法/数据], we quantify [指标名称] using [量化方法], and determine the optimal parameters as [参数值] with an accuracy of [准确率].  
   示例：“We quantify PE with probabilities from Gradient Boosting Trees based on Grid Search (GBDT), and yield GBDT's optimal parameters: [learning rate: 0.2, max depth: 5, n estimators: 300], and achieve an accuracy of 82.4%.”
3. To address [研究问题], we adopt [模型/算法], which is suitable for [模型优势/适用场景] in this study.  
   示例：“To identify the key indicators that influence momentum shifts, we adopt the Random Forest model, which aligns perfectly with our requirements for this process.”

### （三）分析结果时的常用表达
1. The results indicate/demonstrate/show/reveal that [核心结论], with [数据支撑] in [图表/表格编号].  
   示例：“The results demonstrate that Model 1's score predictions align more closely with actual data, showing a similarity closer to 50% than those of simulations assuming no momentum (Figure 7).”
2. Compared with [对比对象], [研究对象] achieves a higher/lower [评估指标], indicating [结论推导].  
   示例：“Compared to the randomly simulated results, our model consistently yields a smaller DTW distance, indicating a better fit with real match data.”
3. It is evident from [图表/数据来源] that [观察结果], which suggests/implies that [深层结论].  
   示例：“It is evident from the DPI curve that Alcaraz's DPI significantly leads over Djokovic in the third set, which suggests his dominant performance in this set.”

### （四）衔接图片和写作的套话
1. As illustrated in Figure [图表编号], [图表核心内容描述], which directly reflects [结论关联].  
   示例：“As illustrated in Figure 6, the DPI curve of both players fluctuates and interchanges frequently towards the end of the second set, which directly reflects the intensity of the situation.”
2. The graph/figure/table [图表编号] displays/presents/shows the [数据类型] of [研究对象], from which we can observe that [关键观察结果].  
   示例：“The table 6 presents the performance of our model applied to three different tennis matches, from which we can observe that the model performs poorly on the acrylic surface match.”
3. As shown in [图表编号], [具体数据/曲线/分布描述], confirming that [模型有效性/结论正确性].  
   示例：“As shown in Figure 10, most of the turning points marked by our model coincide with the actual momentum shifts, confirming the effectiveness of the prediction model.”

## 四、论证逻辑
### （一）如何引出模型假设
1. 基于研究问题的复杂性，明确假设的目的是“简化分析、聚焦核心矛盾”。句式：“To simplify the analysis and focus on [核心研究对象], we make the following assumptions:”
2. 结合领域常识与文献支撑，说明假设的合理性。示例：“We assume that a player's individual ability remains constant during a match because a player's skill level, physical condition, strategy, and mental state are considered fixed throughout the game (supported by previous studies on match dynamics).”
3. 逐条列出假设，每条假设明确“假设内容+假设依据”，确保假设不脱离实际场景。例如假设3：“We assume that the player with the serve has an advantage. Because serving initiates play and allows the server to control the game's pace and style from the outset, historical data and professional observations consistently show that players serving have a higher probability of winning the game.”

### （二）如何说明模型选择的合理性
1. 结合研究目标与数据特征，论证模型的适配性。句式：“To [研究目标], we select [模型名称] because it [模型优势，如处理非线性关系、捕捉时序特征、高预测精度等], which is well-suited for [数据特征/研究场景].”  
   示例：“To predict momentum shifts, we select Random Forest Classification because it excels in handling high-dimensional data and identifying key feature contributions, which is well-suited for our dataset with 15 training variables.”
2. 对比其他潜在模型，突出所选模型的优势。示例：“Compared with single decision trees, Random Forest reduces overfitting by integrating multiple trees, ensuring higher stability and accuracy in prediction.”
3. 引用文献或领域共识，增强模型选择的权威性。示例：“Gradient Boosting Trees are widely used in sports outcome prediction (Dietl & Nesseler, 2017) due to their ability to capture complex nonlinear relationships between situational factors and match results, which is why we adopt this model to quantify PE.”
4. 结合模型参数优化结果，佐证模型的有效性。示例：“Through Grid Search, we obtained the optimal parameters for GBDT, achieving a prediction accuracy of 82.4%, which validates the rationality of our model selection.”

### （三）如何讨论模型局限性
1. 客观承认模型在特定场景下的性能不足，结合实证结果支撑。句式：“Despite the satisfactory performance in [适用场景], our model exhibits limitations in [局限场景], as shown in [数据/图表支撑].”  
   示例：“Despite the good performance in Wimbledon matches, our model performs poorly in the US Open match (accuracy 58%), which is attributed to the discrepancy in court surface material.”
2. 从数据依赖、模型结构、应用范围等维度剖析局限性根源。示例：“The HMM model is overly reliant on historical data, including the performance of established players and match records, which limits its predictive ability for new players or rare match scenarios.”
3. 提出具体、可行的改进方向，体现研究的延续性。句式：“To address these limitations, future research could [改进措施，如补充数据维度、优化模型结构、拓展应用场景等].”  
   示例：“To enhance the generalization ability, future iterations of the model should incorporate factors such as court surface material and venue altitude, which are potential influencers of match momentum.”
4. 辩证看待局限性，明确模型的适用边界。示例：“While the model has limitations in cross-sport generalization, it remains highly effective for tennis matches, especially on grass and hard courts similar to the training data.”
