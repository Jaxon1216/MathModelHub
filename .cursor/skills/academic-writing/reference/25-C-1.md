### （二）各章节核心功能
1. **引言（Introduction）**：阐述2024巴黎奥运会奖牌分布现状与现有预测方法的不足，明确5项核心研究任务，概述整体研究流程与技术路线。
2. **建模准备（Preparation for Modeling）**：提出模型基本假设并说明合理性，定义关键符号便于后续表述，处理原始数据（数据合并、缺失值填充等），为建模提供数据支撑。
3. **奖牌预测（Problem 1）**：核心建模章节，通过PCA降维、LSTM时间序列分析、XGBoost-Bootstrap集成模型实现2028奥运会奖牌排名预测；用非参数检验识别金牌零突破国家；结合Spearman和SHAP分析赛事与奖牌数的相关性及重要性。
4. **优秀教练效应（Problem 2）**：构建DID模型量化教练更换对奖牌数的因果效应，通过假设检验和平行趋势检验验证模型可靠性，输出各国各项目教练贡献系数。
5. **新见解（Problem 3）**：基于前文模型结果，提炼奖牌数时空动态特征、赛事与奖牌数关联、教练投资方向三类核心见解，并给出对应的决策支持建议。
6. **敏感性分析（Sensitivity Analysis）**：验证主成分数量选择的合理性，通过MSE、RMSE、MAE、MAPE及R²等指标评估模型预测精度与稳健性。
7. **模型分析（Model Analysis）**：总结模型在算法集成、不确定性量化、可解释性等方面的优势，指出计算成本、信息损失等局限性，并提出未来改进方向。
8. **备忘录（Memorandum）**：以正式文书形式向国际奥委会提交研究成果，简明概述模型构建、核心发现与应用价值。

## 二、写作风格
### （一）常用过渡词/连接词
- 序列关系：First（首先）、Next（其次）、Subsequently（随后）、Furthermore（此外）、Finally（最后）
- 因果关系：Thus（因此）、Consequently（由此）、As a result（结果）、Due to（由于）、Owing to（鉴于）
- 转折对比：In contrast（相反）、However（然而）、Conversely（反之）、While（而）、Despite（尽管）
- 补充说明：Additionally（此外）、Moreover（再者）、In addition（另外）、Specifically（具体而言）
- 总结归纳：Overall（总体而言）、In summary（综上）、To conclude（综上所述）、Collectively（共同来看）

### （二）句子结构特点
- 被动语态为主：强调研究过程与结果的客观性，减少主观表述。例如“Principal Component Analysis (PCA) was employed to reduce the dimensionality”“The model was trained using 10-fold cross-validation”。
- 长句与短句结合：复杂概念用长句精准表述（含从句、插入语），结果呈现与逻辑衔接用短句简洁过渡。例如“By employing multi-source heterogeneous data fusion and machine learning integration methods, we achieve an in-depth analysis and reliable prediction of the development patterns in competitive sports”。
- 学术性强：多用名词化结构（如“dimensionality reduction”“temporal feature mining”）和专业短语，避免口语化表达。

### （三）数据呈现方式
- 图表优先，文字辅助：核心结果（如预测排名、相关性系数、模型性能）均通过图表（折线图、热力图、表格、小提琴图）直观呈现，文字仅补充说明图表核心信息与趋势。
- 表格用于结构化数据：如符号定义、预测结果及置信区间、模型评估指标、贡献系数等，便于快速查阅对比。
- 图表标注规范：每个图表均有明确标题（Fig.+序号+主题），图表内包含必要图例、坐标轴标签，文字中通过“Figs. X(a)-(b)”“Table X”精准引用。

### （二）描述模型时的固定句式
1. 模型构建：“We develop/establish/construct a [模型名称] to [模型功能], utilizing [核心技术] for [具体任务].”  
   例：“We develop a multidimensional predictive model to analyze Olympic medal patterns, utilizing Principal Component Analysis (PCA) for dimensionality reduction and noise filtering.”
2. 技术融合：“By combining/integrating [技术1] and [技术2], the model achieves [性能优势] in [应用场景].”  
   例：“By combining LSTM networks and XGBoost, the model achieves high accuracy in capturing temporal trends and nonlinear relationships.”
3. 模型假设：“Assumption X: [假设内容]. → Justification: [合理性说明].”  
   例：“Assumption 2: A country's past Olympic performance is a reliable indicator of future medal counts. → Justification: We assume that historical performance reflects long-term trends in national sports development.”
4. 目标函数：“The objective function of the [模型名称] is defined as: [公式], where [符号定义].”  
   例：“The objective function is Equation 7: \(L(\theta)=\sum_{i=1}^{n} l\left(y_{i}, \hat{y}_{i}\right)+\sum_{k=1}^{K} \Omega\left(f_{k}\right)\), where \(l(\cdot)\) is the loss function, \(\Omega(\cdot)\) is the regularization term.”

### （三）分析结果时的常用表达
1. 趋势描述：“The results indicate/reveal/show an upward/downward trend for [研究对象].”  
   例：“The results indicate an upward trend for countries such as the United States and the United Kingdom.”
2. 显著性说明：“[结果] is statistically significant (P < 0.05), suggesting that [结论].”  
   例：“The coaching change in Japanese judo is statistically significant (P = 0.001), suggesting a strong positive impact on medal counts.”
3. 相关性表述：“There is a strong/weak positive/negative correlation between [变量1] and [变量2] (correlation coefficient = X).”  
   例：“Sports such as Swimming, Athletics, and Shooting demonstrate a strong correlation between gold and total medals.”
4. 数值结果呈现：“[指标] reaches X, with a 95% confidence interval of [范围], indicating [结论].”  
   例：“San Marino shows a gold medal breakthrough probability of 84.7%, with a 95% confidence interval of [1, 3].”

### （四）衔接图片和写作的套话
1. 图表引入：“As illustrated in Fig. X(a)-(b)/Table X, [图表核心内容概述].”  
   例：“As illustrated in Figs. 6(a)-(b), the United States is projected to see a significant increase in both gold and medal counts.”
2. 图表解读：“Fig. X shows/depicts/demonstrates that [具体趋势/关系/结果], which indicates that [结论].”  
   例：“Fig. 8 shows the correlation between gold medals, total medals, and various sports, which indicates that individual sports have stronger correlations with gold medals.”
3. 细节补充：“The [图表类型] in Fig. X further confirms that [细节结论], as [图表特征描述].”  
   例：“The violin plot in Fig. 7 further confirms that most of the prediction data for KWT falls within the range of 1 to 2, supporting the likelihood of a zero breakthrough.”

## 四、论证逻辑
### （一）如何引出模型假设
1. 明确假设前提：基于问题背景与现实约束，提出模型构建必需的简化假设，避免复杂无关因素干扰。  
   例：“Given the complexity of real-world competition, we propose the following fundamental assumptions to simplify the modeling process.”
2. 假设+合理性说明：每个假设后紧跟“Justification”，从现实逻辑、数据特性或研究目的角度解释假设的必要性。  
   例：“Assumption 1: Athletes are in optimal physical condition throughout the competition. → Justification: We assume that athletes will compete in peak condition, with performance primarily driven by training and competition, excluding factors such as injuries or external disturbances.”
3. 假设范围限定：聚焦与研究目标直接相关的核心因素，不涉及无关变量假设。

### （二）如何说明模型选择的合理性
1. 问题适配性分析：结合问题特点（如时间序列、因果推断、高维数据）说明模型优势。  
   例：“Predicting the Olympic medal tally can be framed as a time series forecasting problem, so LSTM is advantageous for handling long-range dependencies in time series data.”
2. 对比排除其他方法：说明为何不选择其他模型，突出所选模型的优越性。  
   例：“The signed rank test is employed instead of a one-sample t-test because it is a non-parametric method that does not require assumptions regarding the distribution of the underlying population, which is more aligned with real-world scenarios.”
3. 方法互补性论证：若采用集成模型，说明各组成部分的功能互补性。  
   例：“PCA is used for dimensionality reduction to address multicollinearity, while LSTM captures temporal trends, and XGBoost models nonlinear relationships, forming a comprehensive predictive framework.”
4. 模型验证支撑：通过假设检验、平行趋势检验、交叉验证等结果验证模型有效性。  
   例：“The DID model passes the parallel trends test, as shown in Fig. 15, confirming the consistency of medal trends between the treatment and control groups prior to coaching changes.”

### （三）如何讨论模型局限性
1. 客观列举不足：从算法特性、数据条件、现实约束等角度指出模型缺陷，不回避问题。  
   例：“The computational cost of the XGBoost and Bootstrap methods is relatively high, particularly when dealing with large datasets, which affects the model's real-time applicability.”
2. 局限性影响说明：简要分析局限性对研究结果的潜在影响（如精度、适用范围）。  
   例：“The application of PCA for dimensionality reduction may lead to information loss, thereby impacting the model's performance in capturing complex relationships.”
3. 提出改进方向：针对每个局限性给出具体可行的优化方案，体现研究的延续性。  
   例：“Future improvements will focus on advanced ensemble learning techniques to integrate more variables and improve computational efficiency for better timeliness.”
